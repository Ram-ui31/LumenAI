Privacy Policy

LumenAI · last updated 4 October 2026

LumenAI is a non-commercial student project (an AI photo-editing studio). This policy explains what information the project uses and how. About LumenAI.

Pinterest data

LumenAI uses the Pinterest API only to read trending keywords and their growth figures (for example, week-on-week change), once a day, to suggest popular photo-editing styles inside the editor.

  • We do not read, store or use any Pinterest user's personal information, account, boards or Pins.
  • We do not post, save or modify anything on Pinterest.
  • Trend keywords are kept only as long as needed to show current suggestions and are not sold or shared.

Photos you edit

Photos are processed only to perform the edit you request and are not sold, shared or used to train AI models. The project is not publicly released; it is used only by the project team on their own photos and test images.

Cookies and tracking

LumenAI does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or third-party tracking.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with anyone. We use cloud computing services to run the AI models; they process data only to carry out the requested task.
